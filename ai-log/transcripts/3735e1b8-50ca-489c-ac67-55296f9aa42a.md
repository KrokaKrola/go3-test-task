## Analytics: happy path walk

Anonymous user, `reviewStep` on, consent granted on Plans:

1. `/ee/et/?utm_*` → Entry → Plans: **`plan_viewed`**
2. Select: **`plan_selected`**
3. StartingEid 201: **`identity_started`**, then redirect
4. `/auth/callback?status=ok` → Verifying 200: **`identity_completed`**, then Review
5. Pay → CreatingOrder 201: **`payment_started`**, then redirect
6. `/payment/return` → Polling → paid → Confirmed: **`purchase`**
7. Reload on Confirmed: no event, because the localStorage guard holds.

None of the six is missing on this path.

**Findings:**
- **`locale` is wrong on half the events.** 07 says `locale` comes "from the URL path". The callback pages have no locale in the path, so events 4–6 have no source. It must come from flow context.
- **UTMs aren't in `returnUrl` per 05 and 04.** 05 lists only plan, market, locale and orderId; 04 invariant 5 doesn't mention UTMs. Same-tab return still works through sessionStorage, so UTMs are there at `purchase`. But when the user returns in a new tab, another browser, or with no consent, UTMs are lost at `purchase`. 07 and 02 assume `returnUrl` carries them, so 05 and 04 contradict them.
- **Possible duplicate `plan_viewed`.** 04 invariant 6 re-runs Entry when Back restores Plans from the back-forward cache, for example Back from the eID page. 07 says "once per page load", which doesn't cover that restore.
- **The snapshot may be gone before `purchase` uses it.** On entering Confirmed, 04 clears the open order and context, and the `purchase` snapshot lives in the open order. 07 doesn't say to read it first. A purchase queued before consent must also take its params when queued, not when sent.
- **Reload on Confirmed has no snapshot or trial left.** If the guard is missing (consent granted only after the reload), `purchase` falls back to an estimate. 04's Confirmed "trial note" also can't be rebuilt after the reload, because `trialEligible` may have changed.
- **Pre-consent events are lost on redirect.** Events before the consent decision are lost at the eID redirect. 07 accepts this because "a decision normally happens on Plans", which is an untested assumption. A user who ignores the banner loses `plan_viewed`, `plan_selected` and `identity_started`.
- **Guard reset can re-send `purchase`.** Withdrawing consent clears the guard. Granting again and reloading Confirmed sends a second `purchase`.

## Quality

**ADR-01 doesn't exist.** No ADR is in the repo. I checked the budgets against the rendering decision in `CLAUDE.md`, Q23 and 02 instead: static export, plans fetched at build.

**Checks the slice can't run.** Slice tests are Vitest plus Playwright for the happy path and `payment_declined` (`PLAN.md` 2.7):

**Accessibility rules that can't be tested as written, or conflict:**
