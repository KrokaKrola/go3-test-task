**Files:**
- `.claude/settings.json`: hooks that fire when you send a prompt, when Claude stops, when a subagent stops, before compaction and when the session ends.

- `prompts.md` and `prompts.jsonl`: every prompt, with timestamp and session ID.
- `transcripts/<session>.jsonl`: a raw copy of the session transcript.
- `transcripts/<session>.md`: a readable version with user and Claude turns, tool calls and results (each cut to 1500 characters), and subagent turns marked. Claude's thinking is left out.

Transcripts include absolute paths with your username (`/Users/oleh.kotlinskiy/...`). Check them before you submit.
