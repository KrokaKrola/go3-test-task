# A. Product brief: "Subscribe in three steps"

**Goal.** Raise web conversion from visitor to paid subscriber by 15% by the end of Q1.

**Flow.** Choose a plan, identify with eID, pay, done. The whole thing should take under two minutes. Seventy percent of our web traffic is on phones.

**Plans.** Basic, Standard and Premium. New customers get a 30-day free trial on Standard and Premium.

**Pricing.** Show consumer prices including VAT, per month, in euros.

**Accounts.** No sign-up form. The eID is the account. Existing subscribers who land on the page should be taken to their account instead.

**Markets and languages.** Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania, in Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian and English. Latvia launches one month after the other two.

**Content.** Marketing must be able to change plan page copy, badges and the FAQ without an engineer.

**Later.** We will probably add promo codes and gift subscriptions, so please do not make those hard to add.
